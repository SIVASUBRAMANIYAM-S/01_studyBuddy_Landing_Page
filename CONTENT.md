## 4. The problem

**Eyebrow:** THE PROBLEM

**H2:** Results are decided long before exam week.

**Intro:**
Pass percentages and entrance-exam selections are what parents judge your institute on. They are built over months of steady practice and timely support. Most of that work is still invisible until the marks arrive.

**Four pain points (icon + bold line + one sentence):**

1. **Teachers lose evenings to correction.**
   Checking stacks of handwritten answer sheets takes time that should go into teaching and student follow-up.

2. **Practice papers start repeating.**
   Students memorise the same questions instead of learning the concept, so practice scores stop meaning anything.

3. **Weak areas show up too late.**
   By the time unit-test marks are compiled, the chapter is finished and the class has moved on.

4. **Parents ask, and there's no clear answer.**
   "How is my child really doing?" deserves more than a mark sheet at the end of term.

## 7. Feature spotlights

**Eyebrow:** WHAT MAKES STUDY BUDDY DIFFERENT

**H2:** Built for how Indian schools actually run.

### Spotlight 1 — AI handwritten answer-sheet evaluation
**H3:** Handwritten answer sheets, evaluated by AI.
**Copy:**
Students keep writing on paper, just as they will in the board exam. Teachers scan or photograph the sheets, and Study Buddy grades them in seconds, highlighting every mistake with step-wise feedback. Your teachers see each evaluated sheet and remain in charge of the final result.
**Points:**
- Works with your existing written tests. No need to move to online-only exams.
- Every mistake is highlighted, so students know exactly what to fix.
- Teachers stay in charge of final results. [CONFIRM: teachers can review and change a mark before results are shared]
- Results flow straight into class, student and parent reports.

### Spotlight 2 — A question bank that doesn't repeat
**H3:** A question bank that doesn't repeat itself.
**Copy:**
Questions are generated fresh by chapter, difficulty and exam pattern, so students can take as many practice tests as they need without memorising answers. They have to understand the concept.
**Points:**
- Chapter-wise questions for CBSE, Tamil Nadu State Board, JEE and NEET.
- Practice matches each student's level, so weaker students build up and strong students are stretched.
- Teachers build school tests from the same bank. 500+ ready tests to start from. [SOURCE]

### Spotlight 3 — Mistakes become revision
**H3:** Every wrong answer becomes a revision plan.
**Copy:**
Each mistake is saved with the student's answer, the correct answer, a worked solution and a memory tip. An AI study plan then tells each student what to revise next, and teachers can see which mistakes keep coming back across the class.

### Spotlight 4 — Your institute's brand, front and centre
**H3:** Your logo, your name, your colours.
**Copy:**
Make the platform your own. Your institute's logo, name and brand colours appear across the student, parent and teacher dashboards. Families see your trusted brand, with a small "Powered by Vivodha" mark in the corner.

## 13. FAQ

**Eyebrow:** QUESTIONS

**H2:** What school leaders usually ask us.

1. **How long does setup take, and do we need an IT team?**
   No IT team is needed. Study Buddy runs in the cloud and opens on phones, tablets and computers. We configure your branded portal, student rosters and teacher accounts, and you're live within 48 hours.

2. **How does handwritten answer-sheet evaluation work, and can we trust it?**
   Teachers scan or photograph answer sheets and upload them. Study Buddy grades them in seconds, highlights every mistake and gives step-wise feedback. Your teachers see every evaluated sheet and stay in charge of the final result.

3. **Will this replace our teachers?**
   No. Study Buddy takes on repetitive work like correction, test creation and late-night doubts, so teachers can spend more time teaching and supporting the students who need them most.

4. **What does it cost?**
   Pricing is tailored to your institute's size and the modules you need, so we don't publish a single price. Book a free demo and we'll share a plan that fits your school, along with the pilot details.

5. **Which boards, classes and exams do you cover?**
   CBSE and Tamil Nadu State Board for Classes 8 to 12, plus JEE Main, JEE Advanced and NEET preparation. Content is aligned with NCERT chapters.

6. **Is our students' data safe? Who owns it?**
   Your institute owns its data. It is hosted in India, encrypted at rest and in transit, and protected by role-based access, so each person sees only what they should. We follow the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, including parent consent and data deletion on request. [CONFIRM: same items as §10 data block]

7. **Can the platform carry our school's name?**
   Yes. Your logo, name and brand colours appear across the student, parent and teacher dashboards, with a small "Powered by Vivodha" mark.

8. **What happens if the pilot doesn't work for us?**
   Then you don't expand. The 30-day pilot exists so you can decide on evidence. We agree the measures with you at the start and review them together at the end. There's no long-term commitment.
