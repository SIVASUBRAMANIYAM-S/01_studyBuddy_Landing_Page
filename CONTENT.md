## 4. The problem

**Eyebrow:** THE PROBLEM

**H2:** Results are decided long before exam week.

**Intro:**
Pass percentages and entrance-exam selections are what parents judge your institute on. They are built over months of steady practice and timely support. Most of that work is still invisible until the marks arrive.

**Four pain points (icon + bold line + one sentence):**

1. **Teachers lose evenings to correction.**
   Checking stacks of handwritten answer sheets takes time that should go into teaching and student follow-up.

2. **Practice papers start repeating.**
   Students memorise the same questions instead of learning the concept, so practice scores stop meaning anything.

3. **Weak areas show up too late.**
   By the time unit-test marks are compiled, the chapter is finished and the class has moved on.

4. **Parents ask, and there's no clear answer.**
   "How is my child really doing?" deserves more than a mark sheet at the end of term.

## 7. Feature spotlights

**Eyebrow:** WHAT MAKES STUDY BUDDY DIFFERENT

**H2:** Built for how Indian schools actually run.

### Spotlight 1 — AI handwritten answer-sheet evaluation
**H3:** Handwritten answer sheets, evaluated by AI.
**Copy:**
Students keep writing on paper, just as they will in the board exam. Teachers scan or photograph the answer sheets, and Study Buddy reads the handwriting, marks each answer against your scheme and adds step-wise feedback. The teacher reviews and approves every mark.
**Points:**
- Works with your existing tests. No need to switch to online-only exams.
- Step-wise feedback that students can actually learn from.
- The teacher always has the final say.
- Results flow straight into class and student reports.
**Proof line (small):** Hours of correction saved per teacher per test: [VERIFY]

### Spotlight 2 — A question bank that doesn't repeat
**H3:** A question bank that doesn't repeat itself.
**Copy:**
Every practice set is generated fresh by chapter, difficulty and exam pattern, so students can't memorise their way through. They have to understand the concept.
**Points:**
- Chapter-wise questions for boards, JEE and NEET.
- Difficulty adjusts to each student's level. [CONFIRM: adaptive difficulty]
- Teachers can build school tests from the same bank.

### Spotlight 3 — Mistakes become revision
**H3:** Every wrong answer becomes a revision plan.
**Copy:**
Each mistake is saved with the student's answer, the correct answer, a worked solution and a memory tip. Students revise until a topic is mastered, and teachers can see which mistakes keep coming back across the class.

## 13. FAQ

**Eyebrow:** QUESTIONS

**H2:** What school leaders usually ask us.

1. **How long does it take to set up, and do we need an IT team?**
   No IT team is needed. Study Buddy runs in the cloud and opens in a browser on phones, tablets and computers. We set up your classes, upload student and teacher accounts and train your staff. Most institutes are running within [VERIFY: setup time, e.g. one week].

2. **How does the handwritten answer-sheet evaluation work, and can we trust it?**
   Teachers scan or photograph answer sheets and upload them. The AI reads the handwriting, marks each answer against your answer key or marking scheme, and adds step-wise feedback. Nothing is final until a teacher reviews and approves it, so your teachers stay in control of every mark.

3. **Will this replace our teachers?**
   No. Study Buddy takes on repetitive work like correction, test creation and late-night doubts, so teachers can spend more time teaching and supporting students who need help.

4. **What does it cost?**
   Pricing depends on the number of students and the modules you choose. [CONFIRM: pricing model, e.g. per student per year] The pilot is designed so you can see results before committing to a full rollout. We'll share exact pricing in your demo.

5. **Which boards, classes and exams does it cover?**
   CBSE and [CONFIRM: state boards] for Classes 8 to 12, plus JEE Main, JEE Advanced and NEET preparation. Content is aligned with NCERT chapters.

6. **Is our students' data safe? Who owns it?**
   Your institute owns its data. Each role (management, teacher, parent, student) can see only what it is permitted to. [CONFIRM: hosting location, encryption, data-deletion policy, DPDP Act compliance]

7. **Do students need a laptop?**
   No. Study Buddy works on an ordinary smartphone with a normal internet connection. Students can also use school computer labs or tablets.

8. **What happens if the pilot doesn't work for us?**
